Our committee

Leven Pre-School is run by a committee of volunteers, made up of parents and people from the local community. The committee helps oversee how the pre-school is run, including financial management, fundraising, event planning and support for the staff team.

If you'd like to find out more or get involved, speak to a member of staff or contact us.

Join our team

We're always happy to hear from people who'd like to work at the pre-school. Whether you have childcare experience, a relevant qualification, or you're looking for an early years apprenticeship, get in touch to ask about current opportunities.
