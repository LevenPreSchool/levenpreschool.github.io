Session times

We're open from 8:00am to 5:00pm, term time only, for 39 weeks of the year.

We offer full-day, morning and afternoon options. The start and finish times below give you some flexibility to fit around your week.

  • Full day: start at 8:00am, 8:30am or 9:00am, and finish at 3:00pm, 4:00pm, 4:30pm or 5:00pm.
  • Morning: start at 8:00am, 8:30am or 9:00am, and finish at 12:00pm or 1:00pm.
  • Afternoon: start at 12:00pm or 1:00pm, and finish at 3:00pm, 4:00pm, 4:30pm or 5:00pm.

Funding and rates

We accept government funding for both two-year-olds and three to four-year-olds. We also offer flexible pay-by-the-hour sessions to suit your family's needs.

Snacks

We provide a snack during the morning session. This includes wholemeal toast, breadsticks and other items, along with a wide selection of fresh seasonal fruit, and milk or water to drink.

There's a voluntary contribution of £1.20 per session towards the cost of snacks. It's entirely optional. If you choose to pay it, it's charged termly, and it's the only consumables fee we ask for.
